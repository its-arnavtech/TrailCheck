# TrailCheck scenic hero

Generated with the built-in ImageGen tool, matching the image workflow used for Tri-Realm Eco. Converted to WebP with Sharp for the website. The UI labels it as inspired, AI-generated scenery. All park cards and detail photographs use separately credited NPS photographs.

## Generation prompt

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: ultra-wide website landscape hero for TrailCheck, a national park discovery and conditions app. Create a extraordinarily realistic landscape photograph inspired by Yosemite Valley's Tunnel View: towering vertical pale granite El Capitan on the left, distant Half Dome near center-right, forested valley floor and subtle Bridalveil Fall on the right. Natural clear early morning light, believable granite geology, distant atmospheric haze, dense dark green conifers, blue sky with delicate clouds. Professional full-frame outdoor photography, fine crisp textures, restrained natural colors, no HDR or oversaturation. Wide 16:9 panorama with a clean darker forest/rock area on the left half for white headline overlay added in code; expansive beautiful valley on the right. No people, no buildings, no text, no logos, no UI, no watermarks, no fantasy mountains. This is scenic illustration, not a claim of current conditions. Aim for the realism of National Park Service landscape photography.
